# Language, Settlements, and Agriculture

Genesis models communication, settlement growth, and food production as deterministic domain systems.

- Language keeps a replaceable lexicon and explicit teaching/transmission path.
- Settlements contain residents, buildings, storage, and population-driven growth stages.
- Agriculture models soil, water, crop growth, harvest yield, and fertility depletion.

These systems remain independent from rendering, external services, and LLMs. Higher-fidelity models can replace the domain implementations without changing agent-facing boundaries.
