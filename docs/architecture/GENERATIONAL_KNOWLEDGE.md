# Generational Knowledge Architecture

Genesis treats institutional knowledge as a domain-separated, cumulative civilization resource.

## Flow

`Experience -> Evidence -> Lesson -> Verification -> Institutional Memory -> Education -> New Generation -> New Experience`

An experience is immutable evidence, not truth. A lesson remains provisional until independent repeated evidence reaches the repository's verification threshold. Disputed lessons are excluded from automatic teaching.

## Domain isolation

Knowledge is stored under explicit domains such as `education`, `medicine`, `engineering`, `agriculture`, `finance`, `trading`, `governance`, and `culture`. The repository does not silently move an experience between domains.

## Generational learning

A person's biological memory is not copied from parents. Instead, living people are exposed to verified institutional lessons through `KnowledgeRuntime`. This means a newborn starts with a new personal mind while civilization supplies an accumulated, verified educational starting point.

## Safety and privacy boundaries

Private episodic memories remain on the person. Institutional knowledge contains only explicitly recorded experiences and lessons. Future AI services may consume authorized institutional context, but private conversations and personal memories require a separate consent/privacy boundary.

## Determinism

Repository ordering, verification, lesson selection, and transfer ordering are stable. Capacity limits prevent unbounded growth during multi-century simulations.
